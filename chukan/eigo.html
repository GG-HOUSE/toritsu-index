10　次の英文を読んで、あとの問いに答えなさい。（計22点）

Chiune Sugihara was a Japanese diplomat who worked in Lithuania in 1940. One morning in July, he saw many Jewish people around his house. ①They wanted visas to leave Europe because the war was coming near.

Sugihara asked his government for permission, but ②it said no. He was worried about his family and his job. After thinking for about ten days, he decided to help the people.

For a month, he wrote visas by hand from morning to night. On September 4, he had to leave for Berlin. Even as he got on the train, he kept writing visas and handed them out through the window. When the train started to move, he said with tears in his eyes, "I'm sorry. I cannot write any more."

Years later, in 1968, a diplomat from Israel visited him. The man showed Sugihara an old visa. Sugihara wrote it long ago. The man said, "Thank you. I have never forgotten your kindness." Sugihara's visas saved more than 6,000 lives.

(注) Lithuania リトアニア　Jewish ユダヤ人の　visa ビザ（入国許可の書類）　government 政府　permission 許可　Berlin ベルリン　Israel イスラエル　kindness 親切

(1) 下線部①They が指すものを、日本語で答えなさい。（2点）

(2) How long did Sugihara write visas?　この質問に、本文の内容に合う英語で答えなさい。（2点）

(3) 杉原は、すぐにはビザを書く決心ができませんでした。その理由を、本文の内容から日本語で2つ答えなさい。（各3点　計6点）
